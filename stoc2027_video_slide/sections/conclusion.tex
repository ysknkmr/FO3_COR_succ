  \KEY{Question} & \CoR{} evaluates \FOthree{} by matrix multiplication;
                   is the $2^{O(|\varphi|)}$ translation necessary? \\
  \KEY{Answer}   & Yes: $G_k$ (size $O(k)$) needs $\ge 2^{k-1}$ compositions,
                   even for circuits with complement and converse. \\
  \KEY{Difficulty} & Complement reverses inclusion; symmetric counter-models are infinite. \\
  \KEY{Method}   & A finite colored graph with two witnesses per color pair;
                   $2^k$ recolorings at one vertex; compare gate values by equality;
                   each composition breaks the equality for $\le 2$ recolorings.
\end{tabular}
